## Raw input

Talked to a few early testers. Two of them said the location signal made them uncomfortable even
with consent — they didn't like the app knowing where they'd been at all, even on-device. One
said they'd rather not use the app than grant location.

I think we should drop location as a signal entirely for v1. Photos and calendar are enough to
start, and it removes a whole category of privacy worry. We can revisit location later if there's
real demand for it.
